1. App Related Help

If the app is not opening, a screen is not loading, buttons are not working, cart items are not updating, wishlist is not showing correctly, or profile details are not visible, please refresh the app and check your internet connection first. If the problem continues, contact support with your registered mobile number, device name, and a screenshot of the issue.

App problems can sometimes happen because of slow internet, old cached data, browser settings, or temporary service maintenance. Our support team will check the issue and guide you with steps such as updating details, clearing old data, retrying login, or confirming whether the issue is from the server side.

2. Product Quality Complaints

JFAM aims to deliver fresh dairy products, but if you receive a product that seems damaged, leaking, spoiled, expired, wrongly packed, or different from what you ordered, please report it as soon as possible. Mention the product name, quantity, delivery date, order details, and attach a clear photo if available.

Product quality complaints are reviewed carefully because dairy items are fresh and perishable. After checking your complaint, the team may offer a replacement, refund, wallet adjustment, or another suitable resolution based on the issue and order record.

3. Order & Cart Issues

If your order is not confirmed, the cart total looks wrong, quantity is not updating, an item is missing from the cart, or you accidentally placed the wrong order, contact support quickly. Share the product names, quantity, selected address, payment mode, and the approximate time when the issue happened.

Orders may not be editable after packing or dispatch starts. Still, our team will try to help with possible changes, cancellation guidance, or correction of order details wherever operationally possible.

4. Delivery Support

For late delivery, failed delivery, wrong address, unavailable delivery person, missing products, or delivery marked incorrectly, please contact customer care with your registered mobile number and complete delivery address. You should also mention whether the delivery was prepaid or cash on delivery.

Delivery timing may be affected by traffic, weather, product preparation, staff availability, route changes, or local restrictions. Support will coordinate with the delivery team and update you with the best available solution.

5. Payment, Wallet & Refund Help

If your payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed, UPI payment is pending, card payment failed, wallet balance is not updated, or refund is not visible, please share the payment method, transaction ID, payment screenshot, date, time, and registered mobile number.

Payment issues are checked using order records and payment gateway status. Refunds may take some time depending on the payment provider, bank, or wallet process. Duplicate or failed payments will be reviewed and resolved after verification.

6. Subscription & Pause Help

If you are using a milk subscription and want help with start date, quantity, delivery days, pause request, product change, or subscription cancellation, contact support before the daily cut-off time. Late requests may not apply to deliveries already prepared or sent for delivery.

For subscription support, always share your registered mobile number, product name, current quantity, required change, and date from which you want the change to apply. This helps the team update your plan correctly.

7. Account & Profile Help

If you are unable to login, OTP is not received, profile information is wrong, saved addresses are missing, or you want to update your mobile number or email, support can help after basic verification. Please do not share OTPs, card PINs, UPI PINs, or passwords with anyone.

For account safety, some changes may require confirmation from your registered mobile number or email. This is done to protect your profile, wallet, order history, and delivery details from unauthorized access.

8. What Details To Share

To get faster help, please share your registered mobile number, order date, product name, delivery address, payment method, screenshot if available, and a short explanation of the issue. Clear details help the support team find your order and solve the problem quickly.

If your issue is urgent, call customer care. If your issue needs photos, payment proof, or longer explanation, email support is usually better. Please avoid sending repeated complaints for the same issue because it may slow down the review process.

9. Expected Response

Most common issues are checked during support hours. Simple account or app questions may be resolved quickly, while payment verification, product quality review, refund processing, or delivery investigation may take longer depending on the case.

Our goal is to keep your dairy delivery experience smooth, fresh, and reliable. If something goes wrong, share the correct details and the JFAM support team will help you with the most suitable solution.
